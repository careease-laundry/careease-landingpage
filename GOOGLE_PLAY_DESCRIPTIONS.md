## Full Description

**Careease Vendor - Your Complete Laundry Business Management Solution**

Careease Vendor is a comprehensive mobile application designed for laundry service providers in Australia. Manage your entire business operations from one convenient app, from accepting orders to tracking earnings and managing your profile.

**Key Features:**

📦 **Order Management**
- Receive and manage incoming laundry orders in real-time
- Accept or reject orders with detailed reasons
- Confirm received items and report any missing or extra items
- Mark orders as ready for pickup when completed
- Report damaged items with photo documentation
- Track order status from acceptance to delivery
- Assign delivery personnel and set delivery fees

💰 **Earnings & Analytics**
- View total earnings with detailed breakdowns
- Track daily, weekly, and all-time earnings
- Monitor pending and completed payouts
- Access comprehensive financial reports
- Understand your business performance at a glance

👤 **Profile Management**
- Complete multi-step registration process
- Upload shop images, owner photos, ID cards, and insurance documents
- Manage shop details, services, and operating hours
- Set business capacity and turnaround times
- Pause or resume your shop operations
- View customer ratings and reviews
- Edit profile information (subject to admin approval)

🛍️ **Service Management**
- Select and manage available services (Wash, Fold, Iron, Dry Cleaning, etc.)
- Configure subscription plans
- Set service pricing and availability
- Update service offerings anytime

📱 **Real-Time Notifications**
- Receive instant push notifications for new orders
- Get alerts for order updates and status changes
- Stay informed about important business events
- Never miss an order opportunity

💬 **Communication**
- Chat with customers about their orders
- Resolve queries and provide customer support
- Maintain clear communication throughout the order process

📍 **Location Services**
- Set and update your business location
- Enable accurate pickup and delivery coordination
- Help customers find your shop easily
